Cookies Policy

Information about cookies, browser storage, analytics, advertising technologies, preferences, third-party services, and similar technologies that may be used with Ultra Static SEO Engine.

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that websites or online services can store in a visitor's web browser. They are commonly used to help websites remember settings, support functionality, measure website usage, and provide or measure certain services.

A cookie may be stored temporarily for a browser session or may remain for a longer period depending on its purpose and configuration.

Modern websites can also use technologies similar to cookies, including local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, scripts, and other browser-based mechanisms.

Important: The exact cookies and technologies used by a website can change when analytics, advertising, embedded services, forms, or other third-party integrations are added or removed.

2. Why Cookies May Be Used

Cookies and similar technologies can have different purposes depending on the website configuration.

Essential Functionality

Some technologies may be necessary for basic website functionality. They can help a website remember a temporary session, maintain a setting, or support a feature requested by a visitor.

Preferences

Preference technologies can help remember choices such as language settings, interface preferences, or other configuration options.

Analytics

Analytics technologies can provide aggregate information about website usage, such as which pages receive visits, how visitors interact with pages, or whether a technical problem is affecting the user experience.

Advertising

Advertising services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure advertisements, understand advertising performance, or provide advertisements based on their own systems and policies.

3. Analytics Technologies

If analytics services are enabled on this website, those services may collect technical or usage information according to their configuration and applicable provider policies.

Analytics information can potentially include page views, referral information, browser and device characteristics, approximate geographic information, interactions, and technical performance information.

Analytics settings can change over time. Website owners should periodically review analytics configurations to ensure that they remain consistent with applicable privacy requirements.

Analytics data should generally be interpreted as measurement information rather than a complete description of every visitor or every interaction with the website.

4. Advertising Technologies

Advertising networks may use cookies, tags, scripts, or similar technologies when advertising functionality is enabled.

These technologies can be used to deliver advertisements, measure impressions, understand campaign performance, or support advertising systems operated by third parties.

Advertisement availability can vary based on factors such as visitor location, device, browser, consent settings, advertising demand, account configuration, and the policies of the advertising provider.

The appearance of an advertisement does not mean that Ultra Static SEO Engine endorses the advertiser, product, service, or claim.

5. Consent and Regional Requirements

Cookie and tracking requirements may differ depending on the jurisdiction of the website operator and the visitor.

Some regions may require consent before certain non-essential cookies or tracking technologies are activated.

Website owners operating their own deployments of Ultra Static SEO Engine are responsible for evaluating the legal and regulatory requirements applicable to their websites and audiences.

Where a consent-management system is required, it should be configured so that non-essential technologies are handled according to the applicable rules.

6. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most modern browsers provide controls that allow users to inspect, delete, block, or restrict cookies.

Browser settings vary between Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, mobile browsers, and other software.

Blocking cookies may affect some website functionality. For example, a preference or session that depends on browser storage may no longer work as expected.

Browser controls may also not disable every possible type of tracking technology. If a third-party service is involved, review the privacy and control options provided by that service.

7. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

A website does not necessarily need traditional cookies to store information in a browser. JavaScript applications may use local storage or session storage for certain interface or application features.

These technologies can allow information to remain in the browser after a page is refreshed or, depending on the technology, after the browser session ends.

Visitors can usually manage stored browser data through their browser's privacy or site-data controls.

The exact use of browser storage depends on the scripts and services deployed on the website at a particular time.

8. Static Websites and Cookies

Ultra Static SEO Engine focuses heavily on static HTML generation. Static delivery can reduce the amount of server-side processing required for a page, but a static website does not automatically mean that it uses no cookies or tracking technologies.

Client-side JavaScript, analytics systems, advertising scripts, embedded media, external services, forms, and other integrations can still make browser requests or store information.

Therefore, cookie behavior should always be evaluated from the actual deployed website rather than assumed solely from the fact that the site uses static HTML.

9. Third-Party Services

External services may be used to provide analytics, advertising, fonts, media, forms, APIs, hosting features, security functions, or other website capabilities.

A third-party service may have its own cookies, storage mechanisms, tracking systems, privacy policies, terms, retention periods, and user controls.

The policies of the relevant third-party provider should be reviewed when determining exactly how that service handles information.

Third-party services can also change their technology without requiring a change to the website's visible HTML.

10. Advertising and Affiliate Services

Websites generated or maintained through the engine may include advertising or affiliate-related functionality.

Advertising and affiliate providers may use cookies or other technologies to measure referrals, conversions, advertising performance, or other commercial interactions.

Commercial relationships should be disclosed where required by applicable law or provider rules.

Visitors should review the privacy practices of the relevant advertiser, merchant, or affiliate platform when interacting with an external commercial service.
